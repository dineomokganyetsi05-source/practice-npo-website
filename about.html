What you need to know about us

Mission Statement

Dog Paw exists to make sure no pet goes without care because of cost.
We support pet owners who struggle to afford veterinary treatment, food, and everyday essentials, so more dogs
and other pets can stay healthy and in loving homes.

About Us

Dog Paw is a nonprofit organization based in Boksburg, dedicated to helping animals, especially pets, and the people who love them.
We believe everyone who wants to care for a pet should be able to, and every pet deserves to be healthy and cared for. But for many families,
the cost of veterinary care, food, and basic supplies is out of reach. That's where we step in.

How We Help

  • veterinary and medical care for pets whose owners can't afford treatment
  • Food to keep pets fed and healthy
  • Dog essentials like bedding, leashes, and bowls.
  • Pet essentials for other animals in need.
